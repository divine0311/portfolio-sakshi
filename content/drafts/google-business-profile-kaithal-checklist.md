## Why Google Business Profile matters in Kaithal

When someone in Kaithal searches "salon near me" or "best coaching centre in Kaithal", Google shows a map pack of local businesses. Most taps go to the first two or three listings. If your profile is missing, incomplete or unverified, a competitor with a better listing takes customers that were already looking for you.

A Google Business Profile (GBP) is free, takes a few hours to set up, and is the single highest-return local SEO step for a small business in Kaithal. Here is the checklist I run through for every local client.

## 1. Name, category and service area

- Use your real business name as it appears on your signboard — no keywords stuffed in ("Sharma Sweets — Best Sweets in Kaithal" hurts more than helps).
- Pick the most specific category you can (e.g. "Boutique" or "Beauty salon" rather than just "Store").
- Set your service area to Kaithal, Haryana so people nearby see you.

## 2. Complete every field

- Address, phone and website must match your site and signage.
- Business hours, including festival-season changes.
- A short description that mentions your main service and Kaithal naturally — one or two sentences, no repetition.

## 3. Photos and videos

- A cover photo and logo.
- 5–10 real photos of your shop, products, work and team.
- One short vertical video of your space or a recent job. Profile with photos get far more profile views than those without.

## 4. Collect and answer reviews

- Ask three satisfied customers to leave a review with a photo.
- Reply to every review — thank them and mention the service you gave.
- Never buy or fake reviews; Google penalises this and it damages trust.

## 5. Post and keep it fresh

- Add a Google Post (offers, new stock, festival greetings) every two to four weeks.
- Edit your hours for holidays so you never appear "open" when closed.

## 6. Measure what changed

- Look at "How customers find you" and "Calls" in the GBP dashboard after a month. The goal is calls, directions and visits — not just views.

## The common mistakes I see in Kaithal

Unverified profiles pointing at old numbers, three businesses sharing one address, and descriptions copied from the internet. All three push you down the map pack. Fixing them is quicker than most owners expect.

## Start with one hour today

Claim and verify your profile, complete your category and hours, and add five photos. That single hour often does more for local enquiries than a month of random posting.
