Photo & Video Editor in Kaithal

Attention is won in the first two seconds of a video — and editing decides whether you keep it. I am a photo and video editor in Kaithal creating reels, Shorts, ads and photo sets that people actually watch, for platforms like Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp.

A good edit is felt before it is seen: pacing, sound, cuts that land on the beat and captions that are easy to read on a small screen. Most of my time goes into those details, not into flashy effects.

What I edit

Short video for Reels & ShortsHooks, sound-synced cuts, auto-style captions and trend-driven formats that stop the scroll.
Ads & promotionsClean, direct promos for shops, salons, clinics, coaching institutes and restaurants.
Photo setsRetouching, colour grading and consistency so a brand's photos look like one family.
Longer editsPrecise timeline work in VN for stories that need careful cutting and colour.

Tools I use

InShot for fast promos and quick resizing, CapCut for templates, auto-captions and trend-driven reels, and VN for precise timeline control. I also use AI-assisted editing to save hours on cuts and captions, finishing every frame by hand.

My process

  • Ask what the video must do — sell, explain or build trust.
  • Shape a hook, then trim everything that does not serve it.
  • Add clean text, music at the right volume and consistent branding.
  • Export in the right format for Instagram, YouTube Shorts or WhatsApp status.

Made for local brands

Kaithal's shops and educators do not need Hollywood effects. They need clear, honest videos that look polished and load fast — in their colours, fonts and voice. That is exactly what I make.
